Guided Discovery

1. Why can't we set initial KE equal to final PE?

Compare the Kinetic Energy before and right after the ball embeds in the catcher. Question: What percentage of the initial kinetic energy is converted to thermal/sound energy during the collision, and why is momentum still conserved?

2. Does pendulum arm length L change the maximum height h?

Keep masses and v0 fixed and vary L from 0.60 m to 1.80 m. Question: Which quantity changes—the height h or the angle θmax—and why?

3. Doubling the ball's initial speed

Double v0 from 3.0 m/s to 6.0 m/s and record both trials. Question: By what factor does the final height h increase? Explain using v′ and h = v′² / (2g).

Guided Discovery

1. Two different ways energy leaves mechanical form

Watch the four energy bars during a full run. Question: How is the thermal energy from the collision different from the work done by friction during the slide?

2. How does friction coefficient μ affect distance vs. work?

Double μ from 0.20 to 0.40 while keeping m₁, m₂, and R fixed. Question: What happens to the stopping distance d, and what happens to the total work done by friction Wf? Why?

3. Increasing target mass m₂

Increase m₂ from 8 kg to 16 kg. Question: Why do both the stopping distance and the magnitude of friction work decrease even though the combined mass is heavier?
